# AJS Digital Profile

Use the uploaded AJS Company Profile PDF as the primary visual and content reference. Recreate its 11-slide structure as a responsive web-based company profile. Preserve the information hierarchy, terminology, visual character, section order, and overall brand identity from the PDF. Do not invent company information or create additional sections that are not represented in the PDF.
